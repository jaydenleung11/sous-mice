What the iterations verified

  1. Separated objective, status, context and controls; folded optional instructions and minimap. Fixed return-button pointer lock and stale binding labels.
  2. Blended shortest-path turns, travel-based gait and action changes. Added controlled flips, landing compression, expressive ears, tails and blinking. Smoothed camera follow with collision checks.
  3. Reviewed front and follow footage: lowered food into the paws, leveled feet, attached utensils and trays to hands, and added camera clearance around chair rails and cloth.
  4. Made captured mice visible on the chef's hand, moved oversized food beside the mouse, reduced unreadable sprint cadence, kept lobby start actions visible, and removed unnecessary world geometry detail.
  5. Front-angle heavy-food review exposed a blocked mouse. Widened and raised the follow camera while dragging, then restored distant human sphere budgets while retaining close mouse detail.
  6. A short-screen fixture exposed a 7-pixel overlap with carrying and buff status together. Tightened the objective card and added a regression check for that combined status.
