# Privacy
Sous Mice requires no player account and has no ads or analytics SDK. Nicknames are visible to room participants. The server processes room codes, team/class choices, inputs, match state and random reconnect tokens to run multiplayer. Tokens, preferences and tutorial completion are stored on the device.

The room host checkpoints state for crash recovery. Rooms expire after inactivity; database records are overwritten/cleared on subsequent room activity. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and operational logs under their own policies. Do not put personal information into nicknames. Room codes are invitation conveniences, not strong access controls. No free-text chat or voice recording.

This draft must be reviewed against the final hosting configuration before store submission. Store privacy questionnaires and an owner contact address remain the publisher's responsibility.
