# Sous Mice — A Bistro Caper
Short description: Steal the feast or save dinner in a playful multiplayer kitchen heist.

Gather 2–8 friends in one bustling bistro. As the Colony, sneak through mouse holes, steal food, eat powers and rescue captured teammates. As the Brigade, serve dishes, patrol the pantry and keep a lid on the little thieves. Share a room code and play from separate phones, tablets or laptops.

Features: asymmetric teams, original characters, 6–10-minute rounds, seven classes, bots, guided tutorials, landscape touch and keyboard controls, reconnect support and original synthesized sound.
